Most modern rain jackets are hydrophobic, but they achieve this through two different methods:

1. DWR Coating (The "Beading" Effect)
Most jackets have a Durable Water Repellent (DWR) chemical treatment on the outer fabric. This is a hydrophobic coating that- when working correctly- causes water to bead up and roll off rather than soaking into the fibers.

2. Waterproof Membranes
Underneath that outer fabric is usually a membrane (like GORE-TEX or ngx). These are technically hydrophobic because the microscopic pores are too small for liquid water to pass through, but large enough for water vapor (sweat) to escape.

Comparison of Technologies
For suburban walking, the choice usually comes down to how "breathable" the hydrophobic layer is.
