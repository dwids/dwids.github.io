# Oi Oi Oi Animals

## Basic Ones

Wombats are stout, burrowing marsupials native to Australia, known for their muscular build, cube-shaped scat, and backward-facing pouches. Weighing 20–35 kg and measuring about 1m long, these nocturnal herbivores live in extensive underground tunnel systems (warrens) across southern and eastern Australia, including Tasmania.

Koalas and wombats are closely related Australian marsupials sharing a common ancestor, but they have evolved to fill different niches: koalas are specialised, sleepy, arboreal climbers, while wombats are stocky, nocturnal, burrowing ground-dwellers. Koalas live in trees eating eucalyptus, whereas wombats graze on grass and roots.
