## About this SiC blog and me.

**Ancora imparo** -  *I am still learning. *

That is the motto of Monash University, where I studied back when. And I’ve openly adopted it for myself.

I like to learn new things and then, if I find them sufficiently interesting, write them up here.  Or take things I already know and document them.  In most cases for a ‘general’ audience and on a wide range of topics:

Movies, TV shows, Australian History, Maps, (e)Books, music appreciation (interesting songs), music theory, photography, Internet (WiFi, phones, underlying networking technologies),  programming,  hiking and navigation, photography and video-editing. And a lot more.

To be honest, it’s not a ‘*Please give me Comments*; or *Like and Subscribe’* blog. If I was that needy and insecure, I’d use Facebook more 🙂  

But seriously, it’s more of a ***Journal of Things I Find Interesting And You May Too.***

It’s a split satisfaction model; I’m happy to write it and just happy to know that some of you read it and might learn something.   

This is the **SiC**; Silicon Carbon interface. Where Computers meet People. 

## A Summary of Me

I did science and maths at secondary school, then off to Monash University in 1979 to do Physics and thus Maths, plus one other [[the-worst-week-in-my-academic-life|first year topic]].

In 1982 and during Fourth Year Physics (Honours),  I [[how-i-got-the-job-at-ibm-in-1982| accidentally found out ]] that the gent in my lab - in the previous year  - went on to work for... IBM Australia.

So in January 1983, I started with them. In a Technical Marketing role; aka pre-sales. 

In early 1996 I left IBM and went out on my own.  In late 1997 another moment of sheer luck happened. Two gents from a company up the road - Aspect Computing - presented to us at the place I was working. Their great attitude and the fact it was old fashioned pre-sales made me contact one of them.

Luckily - when at IBM - I had worked with some of the good people at Aspect as they were an IBM Business Partner. We had gotten on very well and remembered me. Things moved quite quickly and in late March 1998, I started as  full time employee at Aspect. Technical pre-sales. And what a great place it was. 

I morphed into doing both pre-sales and some post-sales (aka services).  I believe my excellent manager spotted I was good at creating documents and explaining tech to audiences of all levels...and he quietly parked that away. So when Aspect was purchased and lots of us were made redundant, I went out on my own again. 

A bit later, said ex-Manager - who had also left Aspect by then - contacted me and explained that a huge Ericsson project - with Telstra as customer, rolling out 3G - needed *IT Technical Writers*. I asked him what that was...and he said I had been doing it, but as part of my Aspect job. I trusted him, gave it a shot. And never looked back.

Now, my late Grandfather was [[anzac-day-golden-wisps-of-pale-men|Arthur William Hudson]], so my IT Tech Writing company was *Artwill Services Pty Ltd.*  

I only retired last year (2025) after many fascinating gigs aka contracts. Nearly every one extended beyond the initial period.  I now have more time to delve into my (self described) semi-polymath and pseudo-intellectual sides 🥰
