## 12 Apostles and the Great Ocean Road

I'd tip 2 or 3 days.  I would go west 'inland' from Melbourne to the 'far end' of the Great Ocean Road, say Warrnambool,The hit the coast and travel East back to Melbourne. The 'attractions' get more dramatic as you move east.  So this way, they 'get bigger and better' towards the end of your trip.


![12 Apostles|600](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/The_Twelve_Apostles_Victoria_Australia.jpg/960px-The_Twelve_Apostles_Victoria_Australia.jpg)

So Melbourne > Inverleigh (coffee at bakery) > Warrnambool, (stay?) 
Pop over to Tower Hill (old volcano) and lovely Port Fairy

Then make your way along to Great Ocean road, stopping at all the cools spots with the 12 Apostles near the end of this leg, then maybe stay at Apollo Bay. Then come back to Melbourne etc
