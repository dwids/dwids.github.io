# Tourist Tips for Melbourne

> [!info] Quick ideas for things to see when visiting Melbourne

## Travel and the Sun

Melbourne has:

- An extensive, cheap public transport network; trains, trams and buses. 
- But...very dangerous sun levels 😵‍💫 

So, I would look at getting:

- A [Myki card](https://transport.vic.gov.au/tickets-and-payments/myki) or phone-app.  *Flat fare* system means a 'hard limit' per day on costs.
- Sunscreen!  SPF 50+. Put it on 20 minutes before you go out. I am dead serious, we have the highest rate of skin cancer in the world. 

## Arts and Culture

Most of these are on or near Swanston St (which becomes St Kilda road) and so Flinders St train station.

#### National Gallery of Victoria — International
==free== (except for any special exhibitions)
Huge art gallery that houses mainly **International** (non-Australian) art

#### National Gallery of Victoria — Australia 
==free== (except for any special exhibitions)
Officially the *Ian Potter Centre: NGV Australia

#### ACMI
"Welcome to your [museum of film, TV, videogames and art](https://www.acmi.net.au/)" 
==free== (except for any special exhibitions)

![ACMI|600](https://acmi-website-media-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/media/images/A_wide_shot_of_ACMIs_centrepiece_exhibition.original.jpg)

> The above 3 are like 5 minutes walk from Flinders St station. ACMI is next door to NGV-Australia.


#### State Library Victoria
Magnificent [huge old Library building](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/) in the middle of the city on Swanston st. Has two train stations minutes away. ==Must see== is the main reading room; a huge dome, Also has has art works, permanent collections and rotating collections. ==free==

![SLV Reading Room|600](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/styles/feature_full_width/public/La%20Trobe%20Reading%20Room%20wide%202.JPG?itok=9GRgTt0K)



#### Melbourne Museum
[Just out of the city](https://museumsvictoria.com.au/melbournemuseum/); short walk for Parliament Station or a tram trip from the city. "Experience the wonder of human history and life in all its forms"
 ![Melbourne Museum](https://museumsvictoria.com.au/media/0d3d4dkg/mm_heroimage-1.jpg?width=1448&height=616&v=1dc170a73ebfe70)

## Sport
#### The MCG  (Cricket and Australian Rules Football)
Imagine 100,000 people at a game at the [Melbourne Cricket Ground](https://www.mcg.org.au/). Nice walk from Flinders St station, or tram or train. I think you have to pay to do a tour. You can't just walk in.

![MCG|600](https://www.mcg.org.au/globalassets/global-media/images/mcg-images/home-page/banner-image/2026/afl/afl_26_wc1melbvcarl_348_homepage-banner.webp)

#### Melbourne Park (Tennis)
Home of the Australian Open in January. Basically next door to the MCG. Not sure if it's open for tours. I will check later.
