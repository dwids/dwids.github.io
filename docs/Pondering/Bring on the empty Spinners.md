 # Bring on the empty Spinners

## Cricket - Sandpapergate 2026 🏏

> Quick speculation on how we got here and where it may go. Bullet points, not narrative. As of 2026-11-21 at 7:30AM. I rate my final prediction(s) at 50/50.


#### Early November 2026
Pre-release excerpts from Usman Khawaja's memoir *Sabr* drop, alleging wider team awareness of the 2018 ball tampering

I suggest that Cricket Australia (CA) called in — or already had — a **Crisis Management** team.

The Crisis Management team suggested:

- Ideally CA to be the sole voice and messenger. 
- Arrange an "Independent Forum" BUT CA — and CA only — control the output. 
- There will be no retrospective punishments 

#### 13th November

Pat Cummins holds a press conference in Sydney rejecting Khawaja's claims as "complete and utter nonsense." 

I doubt he would have done this without CA's involvement. It could be the Crisis Team was called in at this time.

#### 18th November

CA announces the independent forum rather than a formal investigation, drawing pushback and debate over whether it amounts to real accountability.

but then...

Cameron Bancroft publishes a large statement/tell-all alleging the whole team knew and **naming** bowlers and coaching staff.  Thus, it seems, nuking the original Crisis Management plan. 

#### 21 November
 It seems the 2018 bowlers (per se, not their reps) and 2018 coaching staff have gone silent since Bancroft's article.  

#### The deadlines: First Test and Squad
The first Test against NZ starts on Wed 9 December.

I expect the Australian Squad to be announced late November, i.e. just over ONE WEEK away


### The Next Ten Days
Right now, I think the 2018 bowlers will be talking to CA.  If CA, behind closed doors, decides that Bancroft's tale is credible — or they see the public support for him is high — those bowlers are in an awkward position. 

Most of the 2018 Bowlers are tipped to retire within a year, i.e. after the Ashes. As so to Crisis Management Plan, Take 2:

> I think the 2018 Bowlers, including Pat Cummins, will announce their retirement within the next few days. They — and CA — will make no formal comment on Sandpaper.  The boys just decided, for the good of the game and to let the team focus on the Tests, to walk away.
