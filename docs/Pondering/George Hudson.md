# George Hudson - Quick Questions

> George was my Great Grandfather. The current 'gap' in my knowledge is by June 1889 he is married - to Sarah, his first wife - and living in New Zealand, with two young children. Come July 1894, all four of them are in Raywood, Victoria, Australia. I can't find their travel records from NZ to AU.  

This is a summary of [[George Hudson as Adult - The Mysteries]]. I am related to George by his second wife, Alice.

## Quick Facts

**George Hudson** (no middle name, it seems). Born 26 May 1859, Upper Heyford, Oxfordshire, England

**Sarah Ann Haycock** . Born 23 Jan 1865.  Nelson? New Zealand

Come 1854, 14 year old George is in NZ. By 1889 he is married to Sarah with two children:

- **Minnie**:  Born November 10th, 1885 in Ngare, NZ.  (ref: Birth Certificate, named as Minnie on it)

- **Raymond Clement** : Born Nov 26 June 1889 at Nelson, NZ (ref: Birth Certificate)


### The Jump in the Records

They were in NZ on Nov 26 June 1889. The next time I can find them is when their 3rd child is born, but in Raywood, Victoria, Australia:

 - **Kenneth** Charles Hudson: Born July 27th, 1894 at Raywood

### The Missing Travel

So, sometime between **Nov 26 June 1889** and **July 27th 1894** they went from NZ to Victoria. For very sad reasons (see [[George Hudson as Adult - The Mysteries]]) I think it was around **1891**

I should add there is some family talk of them coming via Tasmania. But the above linked item says when Sarah was 31 (1896) she had spent:

- 26 years in New Zealand
- 5 Years in Victoria

Hence my c.1891 arrival in Victoria speculation.
